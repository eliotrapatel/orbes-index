THE ORBES GENOME CODE

Each ORBES piece carries its own identity, issued and signed by ORBES, and can be registered in its owner’s name.

  • IDENTITY

    Its GENOME is unique to the piece. A scan confirms an identity issued and signed by ORBES, and its record in the ORBES registry.

  • OWNERSHIP

    Register a piece in your name to keep its warranty, service history and ownership together. Only its registered owner can pass it on, with a transfer code.

  • THE CERTIFICATE

    Share a certificate of your piece with a buyer or an insurer: its GENOME, your ownership and its date, the warranty. It never shows your name or your email.

FOR OWNERS

  • WARRANTY AND CARE

    The ORBES warranty begins on the date of purchase and stays with the piece. ORBES Care looks after your pieces, year after year: inspection, cleaning and polishing.

  • THE CIRCLE

    The news of the house, its invitations and its questions, reserved for the owners of an ORBES piece. It opens once a piece is registered to your ORBES account.

  • THE CLUB

    A tier comes only from the pieces registered to your ORBES account, and follows them at every moment. No place, rank or early access is ever sold.
